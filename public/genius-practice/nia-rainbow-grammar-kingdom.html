1. Meet the Word Families ✨

🐱
Noun — a naming word

A noun names a person, place, animal, or thing.

girl • bird • school • ball
🏃🏾‍♀️
Verb — an action or being word

A verb tells what someone does or is.

sing • jump • help • sleep
🎨
Adjective — a describing word

An adjective tells what kind, which one, or how many.

happy • blue • little • cold

Some words can do more than one job! For example, “watch” can be a noun or a verb. We'll always use a sentence when that matters.
