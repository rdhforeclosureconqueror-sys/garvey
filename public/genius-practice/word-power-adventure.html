🤝 Synonym

A synonym is a word that means the same or almost the same as another word.

≈

Happy and glad are meaning buddies!

↔️ Antonym

An antonym is a word that means the opposite of another word.

≠

Hot and cold are opposites!

🧠 Memory Trick: Synonym starts like Same. An antonym is an anti-word—it goes against the meaning!
