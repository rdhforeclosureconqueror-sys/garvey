👨‍👩‍👧 For Parents — What does Sight Memory practice? Tap to explore ↗

👁️ Visual memory

Remembering emojis, letters, numbers, positions, or colors after they disappear.

🧠 Working memory

Holding a short sequence in mind while choosing an answer.

🎯 Selective attention

Noticing relevant details during a brief viewing window.

🔄 Cognitive flexibility

Switching between questions about position, identity, and color.

How progress works: Each round asks up to five questions in a shuffled order. Children recall emojis; adults recall letters, numbers, and colors. Slide the marker and tap Lock Answer. At least 70% correct answers make a strong round. Four strong rounds in a row unlock the next level. Longer sequences increase difficulty.

Parent observation: Notice whether your child remembers the beginning, middle, colors, or numbers more easily. Try a longer reveal timer when needed. Accuracy and streaks describe performance in this game, not a diagnosis or a standardized measure of intelligence.
