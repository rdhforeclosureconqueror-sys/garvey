Ready to focus?

Tap one symbol, then another to swap them. When matching symbols touch, they burst! More symbols will fall into the tank.

Choose two symbols to swap.

Every 2.5 seconds a new symbol drops into the lowest available space. Tap any two occupied squares to swap them. Adjacent identical symbols (side-by-side or stacked) burst together. Remaining symbols fall down, and pieces on tall piles can slide sideways into lower spaces. The game ends only when there is no room left anywhere in the tank. There is no timer on your moves.
