The meaning of the gate

Gate of Emotion

Emotions are experiences we can notice, name, express, and learn to respond to. The aim is not to stop feeling anger, sadness, excitement, or worry. It is to understand feelings and practice caring responses to ourselves and others.

Daily practice: Identify the feeling → understand its signal → learn from Adinkra wisdom → choose a response.
